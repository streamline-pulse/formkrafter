Measures and registries: a `measure` brick (number + unit in a family, with the base-unit equivalent kept for totals), a `vehicle-plate` brick (per-country formats, formatted on blur) and a `company-registry` brick (OHADA RCCM, French RCS, generic), with their validator formats `plate-*` / `registry-*`.
